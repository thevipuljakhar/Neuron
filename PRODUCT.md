# Product

## Register

product

## Users
One primary user: a sharp, high-agency renewable-energy professional in India (and a close friend it is gifted to — top-percentile analytical mind). Context: long desk sessions, evening review, dense multi-source scanning. The job: form a daily, trustworthy picture of Indian RE — capacity, equities, tenders, policy, schemes — plus the global forces (trade, supply chain, geopolitics, macro) that move it.

## Product Purpose
NEURON is a free, local, daily-use intelligence terminal for Indian renewable energy. It fuses live government data (MNRE, CEA, SECI, ALMM), market data, news intelligence, and user-maintained scheme files into one auditable dashboard. Success = the user trusts every number on screen (traceable to source + timestamp) and finds signal faster than any portal or feed.

## Brand Personality
Esoteric-precise, candlelit-terminal, sovereign. The "LOTM — Above the Gray Fog" identity: deep indigo night, antique gold, occasional violet. It should feel like a private observatory, not a SaaS product. Confidence through density and restraint, never through decoration.

## Anti-references
- Generic SaaS dashboards (white cards, blue accents, hero metrics with gradient blobs).
- Bloomberg-terminal cosplay (information crammed without hierarchy).
- AI-slop tells: gradient text, side-stripe accent borders, identical card grids, glassmorphism-as-default.

## Design Principles
1. **Trust is the product.** Every figure shows its source and freshness; static numbers are labeled static.
2. **Density with hierarchy.** Dense is fine; flat is not. The eye must land on the most consequential number first.
3. **One identity, no sediment.** The gold-on-indigo system is the brand; every new element uses the same vocabulary instead of adding a new one.
4. **Motion conveys state.** 150–300 ms, ease-out, reduced-motion respected. Ambiance (heartbeat, ticker) is allowed but never blocks reading.
5. **The tool disappears into the task.** Familiar affordances (tabs, tables, drawers) executed precisely beat invented ones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast on its actual background. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored everywhere. Keyboard focus visible. Color never the only carrier of up/down (arrows accompany).
